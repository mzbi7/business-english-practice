# All Ears Biz: Connection & Flow 🎧💼

An interactive web application designed for non-native English speakers to sharpen business communication, natural cadence, and workplace grammar. Inspired by the *All Ears English* philosophy: **"Connection NOT Perfection!"**

## ✨ Features

- **Workplace Sentence Library:** Categorized real-world corporate phrases across meetings, negotiations, email correspondence, and small talk.
- **Audio Shadowing (TTS):** Native browser speech synthesis with adjustable playback speed (0.8x, 1.0x, 1.2x) to practice natural rhythm and intonation.
- **Targeted Grammar Drills:**
  - **Prepositions & Articles:** Master tricky combinations like *in Q3*, *on Zoom*, *at work*, and *the/a/zero-article* rules.
  - **Time Clause Fixer:** Eliminate the common mistake of adding "will" inside time clauses (*"When we sign..."* vs. *"When we will sign..."*).
  - **Workplace Shadowing:** Repeat and pace high-impact professional responses.
- **Custom Sentence Vault:** Save phrases heard in meetings, on Slack, or from podcasts, and practice them with an auto-generated blank-fill quiz. Data persists locally via browser `localStorage`.
- **Fast Visual Reference:** Quick-access cheat sheets for the *IN / ON / AT* pyramid, article rules, and the future time clause formula.
